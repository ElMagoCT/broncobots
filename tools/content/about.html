Team mission

We are dedicated to educating students in the engineering and design of robots. We accept all students regardless of previous experience and work to educate and give back to the surrounding community. By creating an atmosphere of Gracious Professionalism™, the Broncobots develop students who care for and recognize the God-given talents in one another.

How the team runs

Student-led, mentor-supported

Students own the work

They set the build schedule, choose the design, manage the budget, write the code and present to judges. Captains and subteam leads are students, selected each spring.

Mentors teach and supervise

Faculty moderators and volunteer mentors run safety training, supervise the shop and drive to events. They do not build the robot. That distinction is the whole program.

Everyone is accepted

No tryouts, no experience required, no cut. The August bootcamps exist so a student with zero background can contribute by September.

We give the program away

Mentoring Loyola Academy FLL teams, running demos and helping other teams at competition are part of the job, not extras.

How we got here

Built by students, handed down

Every year this program is rebuilt by a new group who inherit the shop, the tooling and the habits of the class before them. The Broncobots began as a single FRC team and grew as demand outran capacity — first FTC teams so more students could own a robot, then FLL mentoring so the program could give something back.
