This is what we actually do

Two and a half minutes, a robot the students designed, and a scoreboard. Everything else on this site is in service of these 150 seconds.

  1. Drive on

    Six weeks of design, fabrication and code roll onto the field in a cart.

  2. Aim

    The hood elevates to a firing angle the software team tuned from a distance sensor.

  3. Spin up

    The flywheel charges. Too slow and the shot drops; too fast and it rattles out.

  4. Score

    The shot goes up. A student built the mechanism, another wrote the trajectory.

Team mission

We build students, and the robots are how

We are dedicated to educating students in the engineering and design of robots. We accept all students regardless of previous experience and work to educate and give back to the surrounding community.

By creating an atmosphere of Gracious Professionalism™, the Broncobots develop students who care for and recognize the God-given talents in one another.
