Programs / FLL

FIRST LEGO League

Our members coach Loyola Academy scholars as they begin their journey in STEM — and learn to teach in the process.

Mentorship, not competition

We are the coaches here

Our FLL program mentors younger students as they begin their journey in STEM. Broncobot members guide Loyola Academy scholars in coding, design, and teamwork, helping them discover the fun and creativity of robotics through hands-on challenges.

This is the only Broncobots program where our students are not the ones competing. They plan the sessions, teach the build, debug someone else's code without taking the keyboard, and stand behind the table at the tournament while somebody younger drives. Most of them say it is the hardest and best thing they do here.

Loyola Academy is Brophy's middle-school program, serving scholars from across the Phoenix area. Bringing robotics to those students is one of the most direct ways the Broncobots give back to the surrounding community.

What FLL is

FIRST LEGO League teams build and program a LEGO-based robot to run missions on a themed tabletop field, and also research a real-world problem tied to that season's theme. It is judged on the robot, the project, and core values — and the core values matter most.

What our mentors do

Teaching is the skill

📚

Plan the season

Break the challenge into weekly sessions with a real curriculum, so an eleven-year-old can get from zero to a working robot.

🧠

Teach block coding

Start with block-based programming, then introduce loops, sensors, and conditionals as the missions demand them.

✋

Keep hands off

The hardest rule in mentoring: let them build it wrong, then ask the question that helps them find it themselves.

💬

Coach the presentation

FLL teams present their research project to judges. Our students help them practice speaking to adults.

🎉

Show up on event day

Broncobots attend the tournament in team colors to cheer, and to volunteer where the event needs hands.

👥

Recruit the next class

Many Loyola scholars who do FLL arrive at Brophy already knowing they want to be on this team.

Mentors needed

Want to coach an FLL team?

Broncobots of any grade can mentor. It runs in the fall alongside the FTC season and takes about two hours a week. Tell us you are interested and we will place you with a squad.
