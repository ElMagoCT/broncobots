Leadership

This program is run by students. Below are the people who run it — and the mentors who make that possible.

How authority actually flows

The signal starts with students

Leadership here is not a title on a poster. Elected students set the build schedule, own the budget and make the design calls; each subteam reports to them, not to an adult.

Adults in the room

Moderators & mentors

Faculty moderators supervise the shop, run training and travel with the team. Volunteer mentors bring professional engineering, machining and business experience.

Mentors do not build the robot. They teach a skill, ask the question that unsticks a design review, and make sure nobody gets hurt. If you have a skill this team could use, we would like to hear from you.
