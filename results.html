Results & Awards

Arizona league meets and qualifiers in the fall, FRC regionals in the spring, and championship play when we earn it.

How a match is scored

The scoreboard is only half of it

Autonomous, driver-controlled play and endgame each contribute, and the alliance with the higher total takes the match. But advancement is decided by more than this.

The Inspire Award in FTC and the Impact Award in FRC go to teams that are strongest across the board: robot, engineering notebook, judge interviews and community outreach. A team can advance to the state championship on judging alone.
