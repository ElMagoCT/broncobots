Why the calendar says only “busy”

The Broncobots Calendar 2026–27 is currently shared publicly at the “See only free/busy” level, so the embed shows the correct dates and times but hides event titles from anyone outside the Brophy domain. Two fixes:

  • Signed-in Brophy accounts (@brophybroncos.org / @brophyprep.org) already see full detail — sign in and reload.
  • For public visitors, the calendar owner can switch sharing to “Make available to public → See all event details”. Nothing on this page needs to change — titles will simply appear.
