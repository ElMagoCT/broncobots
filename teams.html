One organisation, three FIRST programs, six competition teams. They differ in robot size, season and how much a single student owns.

Which one is which

FIRST LEGO League

A LEGO-based robot on a tabletop field, plus a research project. Our students do not compete here — they coach Loyola Academy scholars through it.

RobotLEGO, ~9″
SeasonFall
Our roleMentors
ProgrammingBlock-based, Python
Best forStudents who want to teach

FIRST Tech Challenge

Five student-led teams of eight to twelve. The robot must start each match fitting inside an 18-inch cube. Small enough that one student can own a whole subsystem in week one.

RobotStarts inside 18″ cube
SeasonSeptember – February
Teams201, 202, 23737, 26983, 30596
ProgrammingJava
Best forOwning a whole robot, fast

FIRST Robotics Competition

One large team, many subteams. A 120-pound robot designed, built and programmed in a famous six-week window, then driven at regional events.

Robot~120 lb, ~48″ tall
SeasonJanuary – April
Team991
ProgrammingJava, vision, path planning
Best forDepth in one discipline
