About the team

The flagship

As Brophy's flagship robotics team, FRC 991 combines advanced engineering, programming, and leadership. We design complex robots, develop strategic solutions, and represent Brophy's values of excellence, integrity, and community.

The FIRST Robotics Competition season is famously compressed. A brand-new game is revealed in early January, and teams get roughly six weeks to design, fabricate, wire, program, and practice with a robot that weighs about as much as a refrigerator. Then it goes to competition and has to survive dozens of matches, driven by students, repaired by students, and strategized by students.

991 is organized into subteams so that a student can go deep on one discipline while still seeing the whole machine come together. Every subteam is led by a student.

Season rhythm

January to April

  1. Kickoff weekend

    The game is revealed. The whole team reads the manual together, then splits into strategy groups to argue about scoring priorities.

  2. Weeks 1–2: Design

    Prototyping, field-element mockups, and a full CAD model. Decisions get locked so fabrication can start.

  3. Weeks 3–5: Build

    Machining, assembly, wiring, and first code. The longest days of the year happen here.

  4. Week 6: Practice

    Driver practice, autonomous tuning, and failure testing. Break it now, not at competition.

  5. Competition

    Arizona regional events, then championship play if we earn it. Repair, iterate, and scout between every match.
