Join the Team

No tryouts, no cut, no experience required. Pick the part of the robot you want to own and we will teach you the rest.

Interactive

What would you own?

Pick a subteam to light up its part of the robot.

Mechanical

The chassis, the drivetrain and whatever mechanism scores this year’s game. You will learn parametric CAD in Onshape, then actually machine the part you drew.

You learnOnshape CAD, machining, tolerances
Week oneTool training, then a real bracket
Good ifYou like making physical things

Electrical

Power distribution, motor controllers, sensors and a wiring harness that has to survive being hit by another robot at full speed.

You learnWiring, crimping, diagnostics
Week oneBuild a test harness
Good ifYou like finding the one loose wire

Software

Java on the control hub. Autonomous routines, sensor feedback, and driver-assist features you develop with the people actually driving.

You learnJava, git, control loops
Week oneMake a motor move on command
Good ifYou want code with physical consequences

Strategy & scouting

Read the game manual harder than anyone else, build the match database, and tell the drive team who to pick at alliance selection.

You learnData collection, game analysis
Week oneScout a practice match
Good ifYou argue well and like spreadsheets

Business & media

Sponsorship, the budget, the engineering notebook and the awards submissions — the categories that decide advancement as often as the scoreboard.

You learnWriting, design, presenting
Week oneDraft a sponsor letter
Good ifYou want to run the non-robot half

Drive team

Driver, operator, human player and coach. Selected by tryout, and they practise more than anyone else on the team.

You learnComposure, comms, muscle memory
Week onePractice field time
Good ifYou are calm when it counts

Four steps

How to actually join

  1. Come to a meeting

    No sign-up needed for your first one. Check the calendar for the next all-hands.

  2. Do the safety training

    A short session before you can use the shop. Runs regularly in August and September.

  3. Pick a subteam

    Try a few. Nobody is locked in after week one.

  4. Turn in paperwork

    Registration, safety agreement, media release — all on the documents page.
