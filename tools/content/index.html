Team mission & dynamics

We build students, and the robots are how

We are dedicated to educating students in the engineering and design of robots. We accept all students regardless of previous experience and work to educate and give back to the surrounding community.

By creating an atmosphere of Gracious Professionalism™, the Broncobots develop students who care for and recognize the God-given talents in one another. We give those members a chance to explore themselves, to learn and grow as people, so that they can go out and make a difference in the community.

At the heart of the Broncobots is collaboration. Each member contributes unique skills that come together to form one strong, cohesive team. We believe in learning by doing, helping one another grow, and pushing the limits of what students can achieve when given the tools and the trust to lead.

Our programs

Three programs, one team

Every Broncobot belongs to the same shop, the same creed, and the same brotherhood — and picks the program that fits how they want to compete.

FRC

Team 991

FIRST Robotics Competition

As Brophy’s flagship robotics team, FRC 991 combines advanced engineering, programming, and leadership. Competing on the global stage, we design complex robots, develop strategic solutions, and represent Brophy’s values of excellence, integrity, and community.

The 2026–27 season

What a year looks like

  1. August — Preseason

    Recruiting, safety training, CAD and programming bootcamps, and rebuilding last year’s robot to learn on.

  2. September – November — FTC season

    Five FTC teams design, build, and iterate. League meets and qualifying tournaments run through the fall.

  3. January — FRC Kickoff

    The new game drops. Six weeks of design, prototyping, fabrication, and drive practice begin immediately.

  4. February – April — FRC competition

    Arizona regional events, then championship play if we earn it. Pit crew, scouting, and repairs all season.

  5. May — Offseason

    Demos, summer camps, FLL mentoring, leadership handoff, and training next year’s subteam leads.
