Adults in the room

Moderators & mentors

Faculty moderators supervise the shop, run training and travel with the team. Volunteer mentors bring professional engineering, machining and business experience.

Mentors do not build the robot. They teach a skill, ask the question that unsticks a design review, and make sure nobody gets hurt. If you have a skill this team could use, we would like to hear from you.
