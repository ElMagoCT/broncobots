Why the calendar says only “busy”

The Broncobots Calendar 2026–27 is currently shared publicly at the “See only free/busy” level, so the embed above shows the correct dates and times but hides event titles and locations from anyone outside the Brophy domain. Two ways to fix that:

  • Signed-in Brophy accounts (@brophybroncos.org / @brophyprep.org) see full details already — sign in and reload.
  • For public visitors, the calendar owner can change sharing to “Make available to public → See all event details” in Google Calendar settings. Nothing on this page needs to change — titles will simply appear.

The embed is already pointed at the correct calendar ID, so both the month view and the subscribe buttons above are live.
