Recognition

Awards & results

In FIRST, the judged awards carry as much weight as the scoreboard — and often more, because they decide advancement.

The Inspire Award in FTC and the Impact Award in FRC go to teams that are strongest across the board: robot, engineering notebook, judge interviews, and community outreach. A team can advance on judging alone.

Coming to watch?

What a competition day looks like

Events are open to the public and admission is typically free. Here is the shape of the day.

🕑

Early morning

Load-in, pit setup, and robot inspection. Students often arrive before 7 a.m.

💬

Morning

Judge interviews, then qualification matches begin. Alliance partners rotate each match.

🤝

Afternoon

Alliance selection — the tensest twenty minutes of the season — then elimination brackets.

🏆

Late afternoon

Awards ceremony, where advancement to the state championship is announced.
