Students — how to actually join

  1. Come to a meeting

    No sign-up needed for your first one. Check the calendar for the next all-hands.

  2. Do the safety training

    A short session before you can use the shop. Runs regularly in August and September.

  3. Pick a subteam

    Try a few. Mechanical, software, electrical, strategy, business — nobody is locked in after week one.

  4. Turn in paperwork

    Registration, safety agreement, and media release. All on the documents page.

Mentors & sponsors

We need machining, software, business, and public-speaking help far more often than we need money — though we need that too. If your employer has a matching-gift program or an engineering team, that introduction goes a long way.
