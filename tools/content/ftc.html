The rule that shapes every FTC robot

At the start of a match the whole robot has to fit inside an 18-inch cube. Once the match begins it can expand as far as the game allows. Designing for both states at once is the entire puzzle.

  1. Inspection

    A judge measures the robot in its starting configuration. Over by a quarter inch and it does not play.

  2. Match start

    Folded, legal, and holding a pre-loaded game piece.

  3. Deploy

    The arm unfolds past the boundary. Every joint here was a student’s decision.

  4. Score

    Reach the goal without tipping, and do it again forty times.
