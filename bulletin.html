Week of September 7, 2026

Issue 03 · 2026–27 season · PLACEHOLDER CONTENT
Print

This week at a glance

  • Monday — All-hands meeting after school in the shop. Attendance taken.
  • Tuesday — FTC team meetings. 30596 rookies meet with veteran mentors.
  • Thursday — Open build. Mechanical and electrical priority on the practice field.
  • Saturday, Sept 12 — Build day, 8:00 a.m. – 2:00 p.m. Bring lunch or money for food.

Heads up for next week

  • Friday, Sept 18 — Evening build session, 5:00 – 8:00 p.m.
  • Saturday, Sept 19 — Full day event, 7:00 a.m. call time. Ride and food details will go out by email midweek. Families welcome.

Team notes

  • FRC 991 — Offseason robot is back together. Drive practice slots posted on the whiteboard.
  • FTC 201 / 202 — Drivetrain prototypes due for design review Thursday.
  • FTC 23737 / 26983 — Notebook sections one and two due to your captain by Friday.
  • FTC 30596 — Tool training continues. You cannot use the mill without a sign-off.
  • FLL mentoring — Loyola Academy sessions start next month. Mentors still needed — see an outreach lead.

Action items

  • Registration and the shop safety agreement are due for anyone not yet cleared. See documents.
  • Parents: add the team calendar to your phone. Build-season dates move.
  • Order forms for team polos close Friday.

Shop reminders

  • Safety glasses in the shop. Every time, no exceptions.
  • Put tools back on the shadow board before you leave. If the board is not clean, nobody leaves.
  • Batteries on the charging rack, logged on the sheet.
